05 — Straight answers

The questions everybody asks.

My regulars have been coming here twenty years.

And they'll keep coming. But regulars move, retire, and buy newer cars under warranty. Every shop needs a trickle of new customers just to stay level — and every one of those starts on a phone. This is about the replacement rate, not about your regulars.

What's this going to cost me?

Less than a slow week, and there's no big check up front — that's the short version. The honest answer is that it depends on how many services you run and whether you've got usable photos, so we'd rather quote you straight on a fifteen-minute call than put a made-up number on a web page.

I had a guy build me one years ago. Waste of money.

We hear that constantly, and it's usually the same story — he built it, took the check, and you never heard from him again. You're not buying a file here. You're paying monthly for somebody to keep it working and answer the phone. If we stop doing that, you stop paying.

Who owns the site and the domain?

The words and the photos are yours. We register and maintain the domain while you're a customer, and if you leave after the first year we transfer it to you at no charge. Nobody gets held hostage over a domain name — we've watched it happen and we don't do it.

I'm not good with computers.

Good. That's the point. No login, no dashboard, nothing to update. When something needs changing you send a text message and we change it. That's the entire system.

How long before I see anything from it?

Live in five business days. Calls from your Google listing usually start within the first few weeks, because connecting the website link is the biggest thing most shops are missing. Ranking for competitive searches takes longer — anybody promising you page one in 30 days is selling you something.
